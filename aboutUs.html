About Us

Two travellers, one city we love

We are a small team of students, Pavlos and Ioannis, who love to travel, and Thessaloniki is the city we know best. Over the years we have walked its waterfront at sunset, climbed to the old walls, queued for fresh bougatsa and lost ourselves in its museums. This guide is our way of sharing all of it with you.

We are a non-profit project. We do not sell tickets, take commissions or accept payment to promote a place. Everything on this site is here because we think it is worth your time.

What we believe

  • Travel is better when you understand the culture behind the places.
  • Good advice should be free and honest.
  • Visitors and locals can enjoy the same city together.
  • Every neighbourhood has a story worth telling.
