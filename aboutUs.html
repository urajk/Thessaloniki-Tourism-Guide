Discover Thessaloniki With Us!

We are Pavlos and Ioannis, two college students with a shared passion for tourism, technology, and creating things that people actually enjoy using.

Our goal is simple: to help visitors discover Thessaloniki beyond the usual tourist attractions. From hidden local gems and beautiful historical landmarks to the best places to eat, drink, and enjoy the city's nightlife, we want to show you what makes Thessaloniki truly special.

With years of experience in web development, digital creativity, and exploring everything our city has to offer, we decided to combine our interests and create a guide that makes discovering Thessaloniki easier, more enjoyable, and a little more exciting.

We believe that the best travel experiences come from exploring like a local, not just following a tourist map. That's why we're here to share recommendations, useful information, and a few secrets that you might not find in a typical travel guide.

Two students, one city, and a whole lot of places to discover.

Welcome to Thessaloniki. Let's explore!
