Archaeological Museum

Archaeological Museum

Treasures of ancient Macedonia, from prehistoric times to the Roman city. Do not miss the golden Derveni Krater and the jewellery of the Macedonian tombs.

📍 Manoli Andronikou 6, near the International Fair

Museum of Byzantine Culture

Museum of Byzantine Culture

Icons, mosaics, wall paintings and everyday objects from the Byzantine and post-Byzantine years. It won the Council of Europe Museum Prize in 2005.

📍 Stratou Avenue 2, next to the Archaeological Museum

Jewish Museum

Jewish Museum

The story of the Sephardic Jewish community, which shaped the life of the city for more than four centuries, and of its tragic loss in the Second World War.

📍 Agiou Mina 13, near the Modiano market

NOESIS Science Center

NOESIS Science Center

A science and technology museum with a planetarium, interactive exhibits and a hall of old cars and inventions. Great for families and kids.

📍 Thermi, about 20 minutes from the centre
